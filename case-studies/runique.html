Building Runique Store – A Niche E-commerce Brand for Runners

Designing a highly tailored, custom Shopify experience that leverages community identity, payment gateways, and conversion tracking for endurance athletes.

Overview

About Runique Store

Runique Store is a niche e-commerce brand built specifically for runners and fitness enthusiasts. The platform offers personalized and community-driven merchandise such as running logs, medal hangers, tumblers, hoodies, race merchandise, and runner-themed accessories.

The goal was to create a specialized, high-conversion shopping experience that directly resonates with runners and celebrates their endurance milestones, rather than operating as a generic apparel or merchandise store.

Problem Statement

The Challenge

While the running and marathon community has grown exponentially in India, the market suffered from key e-commerce gaps:

  • [!] Most available running merchandise was generic, lacking emotional alignment with runners' personal achievements.
  • [!] Lack of customizable options where athletes could showcase race dates, personal records, and marathons.
  • [!] No dedicated online platform catering directly to running club communities.
  • [!] Difficulty launching and onboarding race-specific product collections rapidly when marathons take place.
  • [!] Founders needed an operations-friendly, highly scalable e-commerce infrastructure with minimal server upkeep.
Solution

The Solution

We built a specialized e-commerce experience on Shopify, focusing on runner identity, clean mobile-first browsing, and automated regional payments.

Key Features

🏃 Community-Focused Merchandise

Tailored collections including structured running journals, medal display hangers, specialized HYROX endurance accessories, and customizable race-specific items.

📱 Mobile-First E-commerce Experience

Designed layouts optimized for mobile devices (which drives over 85% of community traffic), featuring category-based navigation and an accelerated checkout experience.

📣 Identity-Driven Storytelling

Integrated customized brand sections focusing on running milestones, race participation reviews, and community stories that position the brand as an authentic runner-led movement.

📦 Seamless Operations & Fulfillment

Configured inventory trackers, automated invoice generations, localized delivery notifications, and structured order tracking pipelines.

Architecture

Technical System Design

To support rapid scaling and low operational overhead, we utilized Shopify's SaaS platform coupled with custom liquid extensions and local Indian API nodes.

Storefront & Custom Themes

Created custom layouts and Liquid template modifications on Shopify. Implemented asynchronous image styling and lazy loading scripts to ensure instantaneous page loads under mobile network constraints.

Payment Gateway Nodes

Integrated the Razorpay Payment Gateway API into Shopify. Configured seamless UPI, credit card, and net banking options. Implemented checkout callback error logs to prevent duplicate order charges during payment delays.

Analytics & Conversion Funnel

Implemented pixel integrations for marketing channels. Structured Google Analytics 4 (GA4) event trackers to log user drop-offs, checkout step metrics, and campaign conversion rates.

Challenges

Engineering Challenges & Approaches

[CHALLENGE 01]

Designing for a High-Intent Niche Audience

Unlike generic online clothing catalogs, endurance runners purchase items centered around races, achievements, and identity. A default store layout failed to convert visitors.

Approach: Structured collections around specific runner milestones (e.g., "Medal Hangers", "Training Journals"). Emphasized achievement-centric graphics on the storefront and mapped product features to marathon preparation guides.

[CHALLENGE 02]

Mobile Product Discovery Impediments

Athletes visiting the store via social media links had high cart-abandonment rates due to complex navigation structures and slow product filtering options on mobile.

Approach: Refined the mobile UI to offer category-driven filters, shortened the path from landing page to cart to 3 clicks, and implemented one-click UPI checkout panels to reduce transaction times.

[CHALLENGE 03]

Rapid Merchandise Onboarding During Race Seasons

Marathons occur in seasons, demanding rapid product onboarding, catalog switches, and temporary collection page releases within a matter of hours.

Approach: Standardized templated onboarding processes. Created reusable custom layouts for collection landings and automated size/inventory metadata tagging, reducing new launch cycles to under 2 hours.
